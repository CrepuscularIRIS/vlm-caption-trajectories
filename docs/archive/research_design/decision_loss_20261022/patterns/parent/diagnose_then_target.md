# Diagnose the Bottleneck, Then Target It
_id: `diagnose_then_target`_

**Plain alias**. _Find what is actually broken before fixing anything_
**Definition**. Use controlled ablations, oracle substitutions or diagnostic probes to locate the specific weakness limiting a system, then route that finding into targeted data, curricula, intermediate supervision, negatives or a minimal objective change aimed only at that weakness.
**Operational signature**. hold the base fixed → ablate or substitute an oracle per component (or per input, stage, objective term) → locate the bottleneck and rule out the default explanation → build supervision or a change aimed only at that bottleneck → train it first, alone, or in a curriculum, and show the targeted fix beats an untargeted one
**When to apply**. When a system fails in aggregate but it is unclear which component, input, stage or skill is responsible, and the community's default explanation (capacity, scale, missing reasoning, more data) has not been directly tested.
**Evidence base**. primary-tagged n=94, oral 2, spotlight 4, poster 64, reject 24; supporting-only n=76

Note: the success side is thin (6 papers: 2 orals, 4 spotlights; orals are only 13 in the whole corpus). Posters (n=64) are used below as supporting accepted evidence and are marked as such.

## Success conditions (from Oral/Spotlight; Poster as support)
- **The diagnosis overturns the default attribution with a minimal substitution test** — Accepted work runs one cheap, decisive probe (give the model perfect input, remove the assumed ingredient, swap the objective) and shows the field blamed the wrong cause: a generalist failed at metric depth because of input ambiguity, not missing heads or losses; explicit thinking was not the source of reward-fine-tuning gains. Posters repeat the move: the language backbone, not the visual encoder, limited temporal reasoning; perception, not reasoning, limited abstract puzzles; information was present in latents but not decodable. [ids: ObFVZGnSFN, YexxvBGwQM, Jb1YBPqyb7; poster support: sHAvMp5J4R, fGRwRnDVMX, MwmmBg1VYg, p3NKpom1VL]
- **The fix is the smallest change the diagnosis licenses, with no new architecture** — Success-side fixes follow directly from the located cause: rendered markers plus focal-length normalization with plain SFT; per-exit-layer policy training for an overlooked representation choice; reweighting one decomposed gradient term plus a regularizer only on conflicting tokens. Posters show the same economy (un-masking the instruction loss, initializing one adapter direction along a measured gap axis, down-weighting a few located heads). Reviewers accept limited technical novelty when the fix is visibly entailed by the diagnosis. [ids: ObFVZGnSFN, F1ff8zcjPp, Jb1YBPqyb7; poster support: NQSWkmjODD, Umu6IsAUbS, Bjq4W7P2Us]
- **The measuring instrument or data source is audited before the system is** — Both data-centric success papers first ask whether the evaluation or the training source can see the bottleneck: one audits which benchmarks truly depend on perception before ranking encoders; another scales open synthetic data to find where it plateaus and spends scarce human annotation exactly there. Posters extend this to train-test presentation mismatch and static-snapshot evaluation hiding dynamic failures. [ids: Vi8AepAXGy, 5NkfjxMpWe; poster support: 5UbeQDlYDj, QarKTT5brZ, QHKJhiz88q]
- **Controlled one-factor comparisons are paired with open, reusable resources** — When the contribution is a study rather than a method, accepted papers hold everything else fixed per factor and release weights, data, benchmarks or recipes, so the diagnosis is reproducible community infrastructure rather than a one-off claim. [ids: Vi8AepAXGy, 5NkfjxMpWe, YexxvBGwQM; poster support: HVtu26XDAA, EuN5iszF0a, zrH2A1upAo]
- **Mechanistic evidence sits at the level where the fix acts** — Success-side papers bridge diagnosis and fix with evidence at the same level the intervention operates: gradient-angle geometry motivating gradient steering; out-of-distribution intermediate embeddings motivating per-layer alignment. Posters do the same with attention ratios, located heads, and causal mediation on intermediate tokens, often with a training-free intervention as the causal check before training. [ids: Jb1YBPqyb7, F1ff8zcjPp; poster support: 4tsfY0lI1w, Bjq4W7P2Us, E2PFv7ad3p, l1cMErXg1P]

## Failure modes (from Reject)
- **The diagnosis confirms what the community already believed** — Rejected studies isolate a question carefully but land on a predictable answer (fine-tuning beats prompting, a two-stage warm-start-then-RL recipe works, weak low-level perception exists, perturbation exposes a robustness gap). Careful execution does not rescue a conclusion reviewers could have guessed. [ids: 369jumtah8, gXgEHrRTfj, wywgRd1MUQ, INoDyme6wS, oFC7fUsGje]
- **The remedy is a generic off-the-shelf component, not one entailed by the diagnosis** — After a reasonable diagnosis, the fix is standard augmentation, a consistency regularizer, a likelihood penalty, plain SFT on a new corpus, or a borrowed decoding scheme; reviewers then read the paper as "known technique plus motivation" and gains as marginal. [ids: 0Cv0whP7l8, OXPKjZ6iB8, AY3QtM0mQ9, c8QlNuhy2G, agRuIB0EZ2, kCrE9uvxlo]
- **Diagnosis without an actionable fix** — Purely descriptive studies (factorial benchmarks, negative results, disconnect audits) are faulted for "no actionable takeaway" or "no reusable training method", even when the analysis is clean. [ids: oFC7fUsGje, XdLgOm5giq, pnhstQ7Dtd, r8e7hBhWSG]
- **The causal claim outruns the controls** — Bottlenecks are inferred from residuals, ablation heuristics or correlations, and compute, data or model-family confounds are left open, so the located cause is not shown to be the cause. [ids: AY3QtM0mQ9, OXPKjZ6iB8, dtaNk0sG8E, r8e7hBhWSG, 6ADnEk90R2]
- **The diagnostic instrument itself is unvalidated** — Benchmarks or feature rankings used to locate the weakness are noisy, model-generated, filtered by weak models, or only ~63% human-answerable, so the diagnosis inherits the instrument's doubt. [ids: Rc8z5wLzBF, x07rHuChwF, EoTIlDT0Tr, agRuIB0EZ2, JUX7x5ync4, LCk3umTAXx]
- **Gains cannot be attributed to the targeted structure rather than more data** — When the targeted data or auxiliary stage is not compared against matched untargeted data, reviewers suspect volume, teacher strength or extra compute explains the improvement. [ids: J0eNXpnrc7, LCk3umTAXx, xreOs2yjqf, OXPKjZ6iB8]
- **One model, one setup, one capability** — Mechanistic or diagnostic claims rest on a single model and environment, making the located bottleneck look like an artifact of that setup. [ids: UXKYpRTzhY, XdLgOm5giq, 6ADnEk90R2, dtaNk0sG8E, r8e7hBhWSG]

## Oral vs Reject gap
The success side (2 orals + 4 spotlights) differs from rejects in two linked moves. First, success papers' diagnoses overturn a default belief with a decisive minimal test (input ambiguity rather than missing capacity; thinking not needed; gradients near-orthogonal; a "fixed" pipeline choice is an attack surface), whereas rejected diagnoses mostly confirm expected effects or infer the cause from residuals and correlations. Second, success-side fixes are visibly entailed by the diagnosis and are minimal, so reviewers forgive "limited technical novelty"; rejected fixes are generic components (augmentation, consistency or likelihood regularizers, plain SFT on a new set) that could have been proposed without the diagnosis, or no fix is offered at all. Data-centric successes add a third move rejects lack: they audit the instrument or data source first and release open resources. Note that the reject/poster boundary is noisy for this parent: four rejected papers reappear later as posters with largely the same content (AY3QtM0mQ9→0tdnyH7uXp, oFC7fUsGje→ANbnxDfcAD, XdLgOm5giq→sfVahQ4EWP, EoTIlDT0Tr→tAKbMv3sf7), so the gap is clearest at success vs reject, not poster vs reject. With only 2 true orals (ObFVZGnSFN, Vi8AepAXGy), the oral-only version of this gap cannot be assessed, although both are consistent with it.

## Reviewer expectations
- **A finding that contradicts a widely held belief, shown with clean diagnostics, is the main reason to accept** _(source: oral reviews)_ [ids: ObFVZGnSFN, YexxvBGwQM, F1ff8zcjPp]
- **Controlled, rigorous single-factor analysis with open release of models, data and recipes is valued above architectural novelty** _(source: oral reviews)_ [ids: Vi8AepAXGy, 5NkfjxMpWe, YexxvBGwQM]
- **Show the hypothesized bottleneck is the bottleneck: the step from diagnosis to "prioritize this component" must be decisively validated, not assumed** _(source: both)_ [ids: Jb1YBPqyb7, dtaNk0sG8E, AY3QtM0mQ9, r8e7hBhWSG]
- **Replicate across model families and scales; single-backbone evidence is the most common concern on both sides** _(source: both)_ [ids: Jb1YBPqyb7, YexxvBGwQM, 6ADnEk90R2, UXKYpRTzhY, r8e7hBhWSG]
- **Novelty beyond known techniques or predictable conclusions is demanded; success papers face the same complaint but survive it via a surprising finding** _(source: both)_ [ids: Vi8AepAXGy, F1ff8zcjPp, 369jumtah8, 0Cv0whP7l8, wywgRd1MUQ, gXgEHrRTfj]
- **Match compute and data against untargeted baselines so the gain is attributable to the targeted fix** _(source: reject reviews)_ [ids: OXPKjZ6iB8, J0eNXpnrc7, LCk3umTAXx]
- **Provide an actionable remedy or reusable method, not only a diagnosis** _(source: reject reviews)_ [ids: oFC7fUsGje, XdLgOm5giq, pnhstQ7Dtd]
- **Validate the diagnostic benchmark or ranking itself (human checks, answerability, filtering quality, contamination)** _(source: reject reviews)_ [ids: Rc8z5wLzBF, x07rHuChwF, EoTIlDT0Tr, agRuIB0EZ2]

## Cognitive barriers
- The default explanation (capacity, scale, missing reasoning, too little data) is so plausible that nobody runs the cheap test that would falsify it; locating the real bottleneck requires deliberately giving the system the "perfect" version of one component and watching the failure vanish (ObFVZGnSFN, sHAvMp5J4R, fGRwRnDVMX, MwmmBg1VYg).
- Aggregate scores hide where a system fails; the bottleneck only becomes visible once evaluation is decomposed into matched variants, reversed orderings or with/without conditions that separate sub-skills (dtaNk0sG8E, QHKJhiz88q, iTWu7ziS5z, J76cCYTJub).
- Inherited defaults are treated as fixed engineering details rather than variables: which encoder layer feeds the backbone, whether the instruction is masked from the loss, how adapters are initialized, which benchmarks measure perception (F1ff8zcjPp, NQSWkmjODD, Umu6IsAUbS, Vi8AepAXGy).
- The salient ingredient of a successful recipe is assumed to be its cause (explicit thinking, latent tokens, the optimization algorithm), so isolating it and finding it inert or secondary is counter-intuitive (YexxvBGwQM, l1cMErXg1P, tP01GCr0nS, nvnkuqWuu3).
- Some bottlenecks live only in internals (attention allocation, gradient geometry, a few heads) and leave no trace in outputs, so a purpose-built internal metric is needed before any targeted fix can be designed (4tsfY0lI1w, Jb1YBPqyb7, Bjq4W7P2Us).
