# Repair a Degenerate Learning Signal
_id: `repair_degenerate_signal`_

**Plain alias**. _When training goes quiet because every attempt scores the same, find out why and give the learner something to learn from again_
**Definition**. Detect when sparse or uniform rewards collapse the learning signal and restore it by filtering uninformative groups, injecting expert or intrinsic signals, anchoring baselines, or rescaling advantages.
**Operational signature**. diagnose vanishing/degenerate advantage → identify its cause (uniform groups, rarity, no exploration) → inject or rescale signal → preserve exploration while training
**When to apply**. When reinforcement learning stalls because most rollouts receive identical or zero reward.
**Evidence base**. primary-tagged n=49, oral 1, spotlight 2, poster 34, reject 12; supporting-only n=28. The success side has three papers: one oral (ZwCVFBFUFb) and two spotlights (4oYxzssbVg, zWStW3hrfW). Posters are used as supporting accepted evidence throughout and are marked as such. The whole corpus has only 13 orals, so a single oral here carries no tier-specific weight.

## Success conditions (from Oral/Spotlight; Poster as support)
- **The pathology is named, measured and shown to be real before it is fixed.** The spotlight 4oYxzssbVg diagnoses that group-normalized advantages silently go to zero and shrink the effective batch; zWStW3hrfW identifies "curiosity drain", where a quickly overfitting projector exhausts the intrinsic signal early. Posters measure advantage collapsing and rollout silencing over training (mYP33u1QBK), branching-budget concentration (Zk0qjcqjV9), and per-token input dependency (bRA4lVWJVQ). Reviewers credit the diagnosis as the insight, not only the fix. [ids: 4oYxzssbVg, zWStW3hrfW, mYP33u1QBK, Zk0qjcqjV9, bRA4lVWJVQ]
- **The repair is matched to the identified cause, not a generic boost.** The oral ZwCVFBFUFb traces uneven performance to domain rarity and within-domain difficulty and rescales the normalized reward along exactly those two axes; 4oYxzssbVg answers vanishing advantages by replaying past high-advantage samples; zWStW3hrfW answers signal drain by periodically re-initializing the projector. Posters trace sparse reward to retrieval noise and turn that noise into a difficulty knob (TH1Tgbjkm7), and switch between imitation and exploration according to whether the sampled group contains any correct answer (qFDju6ahkk). [ids: ZwCVFBFUFb, 4oYxzssbVg, zWStW3hrfW, TH1Tgbjkm7, qFDju6ahkk]
- **The fix is a small plug-in to an existing objective, leaving reward, architecture and pipeline intact.** ZwCVFBFUFb is a critic-free extension of the group-relative objective; zWStW3hrfW is plug-and-play for existing agent pipelines; posters stress minimal code change (5wxyCidRsK), no change to the reward or architecture (mYP33u1QBK), and no extra training cost or change of objective (9zD2i7YRot). [ids: ZwCVFBFUFb, zWStW3hrfW, 5wxyCidRsK, mYP33u1QBK, 9zD2i7YRot]
- **The true target is preserved; signal is borrowed or redistributed, never substituted with an exploitable proxy.** 4oYxzssbVg reuses the policy's own real high-advantage outcomes, and reviewers note this beats pure filtering. Posters keep sparse binary success as the target while diffusing it to similar states, because dense proxy gains overvalue negligible edits (aoUqzNEKpA); mask truncated rollouts instead of penalizing them, which would bias toward short answers (Zp2y9O3wEj); and use noisy-input rollouts only for the baseline while updating on clean inputs (9zD2i7YRot). [ids: 4oYxzssbVg, aoUqzNEKpA, Zp2y9O3wEj, 9zD2i7YRot]
- **Every component is ablated and the fix is shown across scales or domains, with released artifacts.** 4oYxzssbVg covers 7B to 72B with thorough ablations and released data and code; ZwCVFBFUFb ablates each component and releases weights and traces over several clinical modalities; zWStW3hrfW ablates the momentum target, projector rejuvenation and reward weight across heterogeneous tasks. Posters add 32B runs and rollout-matched baselines (mYP33u1QBK) and 7B/32B results (bRA4lVWJVQ). [ids: 4oYxzssbVg, ZwCVFBFUFb, zWStW3hrfW, mYP33u1QBK, bRA4lVWJVQ]

## Failure modes (from Reject)
- **A known remedy is transplanted with little new.** Rejects include classic importance reweighting plus clustering (X7oBvgAeuh), dynamic sampling and leave-one-out advantages taken from language-model RL (oXYZHg7HiZ), answer-prefix curricula that overlap reverse-curriculum work (3rZh4QeNyt), a possible special case of Monte Carlo value estimation (Qr9FAtcpeR), tricks layered on existing grounding RL (8H1HmGH8ua), and an incremental self-certainty modulation (5FSfl8ZPQf). [ids: X7oBvgAeuh, oXYZHg7HiZ, 3rZh4QeNyt, Qr9FAtcpeR, 8H1HmGH8ua, 5FSfl8ZPQf]
- **The fix does not beat, or is never compared with, the simplest alternative repair.** Examples: not clearly better than a simple noisy-rollout baseline, with no comparison to removing the normalizer or adding rollouts (NFxsP6PadP); dense-loss alternatives left uncompared (8H1HmGH8ua); stronger recent objective variants missing (Qr9FAtcpeR); reward-only versus penalty-only ablations missing (5FSfl8ZPQf). [ids: NFxsP6PadP, 8H1HmGH8ua, Qr9FAtcpeR, 5FSfl8ZPQf]
- **The injected signal gives the method privileged help that the baselines lack, so the gain is confounded.** Examples: answer prefixes for the method while baselines start cold (3rZh4QeNyt); proprietary teacher responses pooled in without discussing near-identical off-policy guidance work (E0GbfWBCpD); a reward model pre-trained on ground-truth rewards under a "zero-shot" framing (3LGjvzYgdt); training on a new dataset the baselines never saw (Ovmvu3IZAp). [ids: 3rZh4QeNyt, E0GbfWBCpD, 3LGjvzYgdt, Ovmvu3IZAp]
- **The pathology is asserted rather than measured, so the premise is unsupported.** Examples: the claim that outlier groups matter is never shown, and the difficulty proxies are unvalidated (NFxsP6PadP); task interference is neither defined nor quantified (5FSfl8ZPQf); efficiency causes are claimed without controlled benchmarking (k6AJ1N7BA2); the discovered groups are not analyzed (X7oBvgAeuh). [ids: NFxsP6PadP, 5FSfl8ZPQf, k6AJ1N7BA2, X7oBvgAeuh]
- **Many patches are stacked, each with a small gain, and none is attributable.** Examples: four modules with 1.5–2 point individual gains (Aobvdp3XmP); system choices conflated with algorithm and data choices (k6AJ1N7BA2); three patches with incomplete ablations and confounded data scale (8H1HmGH8ua). [ids: Aobvdp3XmP, k6AJ1N7BA2, 8H1HmGH8ua]
- **Validation is too narrow to show generality.** Examples: simulation only, with two models (oXYZHg7HiZ); one task with degradation elsewhere (Ovmvu3IZAp); one small model family (Qr9FAtcpeR); simple control environments with hand-crafted priors (3LGjvzYgdt). [ids: oXYZHg7HiZ, Ovmvu3IZAp, Qr9FAtcpeR, 3LGjvzYgdt]

## Oral vs Reject gap
Comparing the success side (one oral and two spotlights) with the 12 rejects, the difference lies less in the repair mechanism than in how the repair is justified. The success papers make the collapse visible: a silent stall from vanishing advantages, an intrinsic reward that drains, a normalized reward that drowns rare domains. Each is then answered with one cause-matched change that plugs into the existing objective, is ablated component by component, and is shown across scales or domains (7B–72B; images, ECG and text; heterogeneous agent tasks). The rejects typically transplant a known remedy (reweighting, curricula, dynamic sampling, prefix reveal) without first measuring the pathology. They also skip the simplest competing repair, stack several patches with small unattributable gains, or give the method privileged signal (answer prefixes, stronger-teacher responses, ground-truth pre-training) that the baselines lack. A same-paper pair in the dossier shows the line directly: difficulty-aware group normalization was rejected when it failed to beat a simple noisy-rollout baseline and its premise stayed unshown (NFxsP6PadP). It was accepted as a poster once it showed consistent gains over GRPO and DAPO with ablations of both regrouping signals (jyOgpu5wfC). With only one true oral (n_oral = 1 < 3), the oral-only version of this gap cannot be assessed.

## Reviewer expectations
_"Oral reviews" below means the success-side reviews (the one oral plus the two spotlights); poster reviews are cited where they repeat the point._
- **Credit goes to a clear diagnosis tied to a principled fix. Reviewers single out the identification of the failure mode as the valuable insight.** _(source: oral reviews)_ [ids: 4oYxzssbVg, ZwCVFBFUFb, zWStW3hrfW]
- **Report seeds, error bars and significance. Their absence is raised even for the oral and the spotlights, and it sinks small-gain rejects.** _(source: both)_ [ids: ZwCVFBFUFb, zWStW3hrfW, 5FSfl8ZPQf, NFxsP6PadP, jyOgpu5wfC]
- **Compare against the simplest competing repairs and the closest prior variant, such as removing the normalizer, adding rollouts, other group-relative variants, or single-modality curiosity baselines.** _(source: both)_ [ids: zWStW3hrfW, NFxsP6PadP, 8H1HmGH8ua, Qr9FAtcpeR, E0GbfWBCpD, XN7YWDeFeC]
- **Show directly that the signal was repaired, for example advantage-collapse or reward-variance curves and the effect of buffer size, not only end accuracy.** _(source: both)_ [ids: 4oYxzssbVg, tjXtcZjIgQ, kMPII4XbKC, NFxsP6PadP]
- **Check that the injected signal does not itself create bias or hacking: stale off-policy replay, artificial non-zero advantages, noisy-state attraction, external samples dominating the group.** _(source: both)_ [ids: 4oYxzssbVg, zWStW3hrfW, XN7YWDeFeC, E0GbfWBCpD, FxiO8RTOsq]
- **Give sensitivity analyses for every new weight, threshold or schedule the repair introduces.** _(source: both)_ [ids: ZwCVFBFUFb, zWStW3hrfW, 3rZh4QeNyt, NFxsP6PadP, Zk0qjcqjV9]
- **Account for compute fairly, with matched rollout budgets and wall-clock (not per-step) cost, since many repairs sample extra rollouts or add forward passes.** _(source: both)_ [ids: zWStW3hrfW, k6AJ1N7BA2, Qr9FAtcpeR, mYP33u1QBK, qKXYEg00eH]

## Cognitive barriers
- Collapse looks like ordinary reward sparsity or convergence. It becomes visible as a normalization or batch-composition artifact only when group-level statistics are inspected over training (XN7YWDeFeC, 4oYxzssbVg, mYP33u1QBK, Zk0qjcqjV9).
- Inherited defaults are treated as fixed stabilizers rather than tunable choices: clipping, a uniform divergence penalty, the per-prompt group, and broadcasting one outcome reward to every token (2YPypUwWIv, 8H1HmGH8ua, kMPII4XbKC, bRA4lVWJVQ, H76PMm7hf2).
- Augmentation and expert data are seen as supervised-learning tools or as off-policy imitation, not as ways to restore variance or calibrate updates inside an on-policy group (9zD2i7YRot, kMPII4XbKC, 5wxyCidRsK, E88ye5tTF8).
- The obvious fix introduces its own bias. Dense proxies overvalue negligible changes, zero reward for truncated runs pushes toward shortness, and dropping the normalizer or enlarging groups is costly or blunt (aoUqzNEKpA, Zp2y9O3wEj, jyOgpu5wfC).
- A critic-free normalized objective is assumed neutral across data sources, which hides that it amplifies imbalance between domains, subgroups or easy and hard tasks (ZwCVFBFUFb, X7oBvgAeuh, U80CWouffM).
