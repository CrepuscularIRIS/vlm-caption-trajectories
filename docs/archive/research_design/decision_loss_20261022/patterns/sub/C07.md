# C07 — Diagnose Failures, Then Target Training
_parent: **Diagnose the Bottleneck, Then Target It** (`diagnose_then_target`)_ · secondary: none · n=44 (oral 1, spotlight 2, poster 28, reject 13)

## Tactical pattern

The shared move is to hold a base system fixed, run a controlled diagnostic that localizes why it fails, and then route that localized finding into targeted training (data, curriculum, negatives, an auxiliary stage or a narrow intervention) rather than into undirected scaling. The diagnostic takes recurring forms: single-factor ablation of every design choice in a recipe (component, objective, data category, mixing ratio) on a fixed footing, often after first auditing which evaluation instruments actually depend on the component under study; scaling an open data source until a capability plateaus, which marks where scarce targeted supervision should go; with-versus-without paired evaluation of the same checkpoint (with and without an auxiliary signal, a tool, or one input channel) partitioned by initial correctness so every change maps to a named mechanism; intervention on each link of an input→intermediate→output chain to see whether a stated intermediate is causally used; counterfactual-edit attribution that ranks internal components by effect on the failure; probes showing information is present internally while training exposure predicts accuracy; and per-round category-level failure attribution of the current checkpoint. The targeting step then turns the finding into training: data synthesized for the diagnosed weak categories, a curriculum that routes failures to simpler variants along the responsible axis, labels derived from the system's own with/without discrepancy, dense per-element supervision that makes a diagnosed shortcut unprofitable, or an intervention restricted to the culprit components. The headline is the diagnosis itself plus evidence that targeting it beats an equal-budget untargeted alternative.

## Step-by-Step

1. Fix the footing before measuring anything. Hold the base system, data volume and training regime fixed, and audit the evaluation instruments so the ones you rely on genuinely depend on the component or capability under study (the oral-tier study began by discarding evaluations that could be passed without the studied input). Rejected diagnostic studies compared settings with mismatched compute or unmatched baselines, so any gain could be extra training rather than the diagnosed cause.
2. Run a diagnostic that isolates one cause and rules out its rivals. Vary one factor at a time, pair with-versus-without evaluations of the same checkpoint, intervene separately on each causal link, or scale a data source to expose the exact plateau; then name the mechanism each measured change corresponds to. Rejected papers inferred causation from ablation heuristics or correlations, attributed failures to a residual term that could absorb incompletely probed errors, or made causal claims (a misalignment, a robustness-to-reasoning link) stronger than the analysis supported.
3. Show the diagnosis is new and not a restatement. The finding should overturn or sharpen a face-value reading — an inert intermediate the field assumed was used, a capability gain that is really reduced harm, information present internally but not decoded, a scaling failure that is really proxy-objective hacking. A diagnosis that re-derives a known observation, or a factorial comparison grid that only describes which settings win without explaining why, was declined as descriptive.
4. Route the finding into targeted supervision and compare against untargeted alternatives at equal budget. Synthesize data for exactly the diagnosed categories, direct scarce human annotation at the measured plateau, convert with/without discrepancies into routing labels, or restrict an intervention to the culprit components; then include uniform-sampling, generic hard-example mining or same-data baselines so the gain is attributable to targeting. Reviewers discounted loops whose "diagnosis" reduced to hand-designed category reweighting, and loops in which a stronger external generator does the work (making it distillation rather than diagnosis-driven improvement).
5. Validate the targeted fix beyond the diagnostic setting and release the instruments. Test more than one base system and more than one domain or source, verify automatically produced labels (report noise or human agreement), check train/evaluation overlap, and show general capability is preserved; release data, code and diagnostic tools when the contribution is a recipe. Rejects frequently rested on a single domain or data source, a single model family, noisy specialist-model labels with no robustness check, or effects that faded on stronger backbones.

## Differentiation within parent

- vs **Isolate Weak Sub-Skill, Supervise Intermediates** (C02): that sibling's targeting step is to expand the diagnosed low-level sub-skill into explicit intermediate representations (often generator- or simulator-supplied) trained before or alongside the downstream task, so the output of the method is a new intermediate. Here the targeting step stays at the level of training inputs and schedule — targeted data, curricula, negatives, routing labels or a narrow component intervention — and the diagnosis often concerns the recipe, the objective, an input channel or the system's own failure categories rather than one perceptual sub-skill.

## When to pick this one

Reach for this when a system fails in aggregate, you can hold it fixed and measure it under controlled with/without or one-factor-at-a-time conditions, and you expect the cause to be specific enough that targeted data or a narrow intervention will beat an equal-budget untargeted alternative. If the only available diagnosis is a correlation, or the "targeting" is just reweighting categories you already suspected, the contribution will read as a descriptive report or a generic hard-example loop.

## Tactical failure mode

Papers attempting this tactic fail most often by stopping at description: a factorial grid or comparative study whose takeaways are not articulated, not explained mechanistically, and not turned into a fix, so reviewers see a technical report. A second recurring shape is an unsupported causal attribution — ablation heuristics, residual terms or correlations presented as causes, with confounds (data quality, compute, prompt format) left uncontrolled. A third is a fix that is known augmentation, regularization or data-evolution machinery bolted onto the diagnosis with marginal gains and no equal-budget baseline. Finally, narrow scope (one domain, one data source, one model family, noisy auto-labels) caps even sound diagnoses. Self-check: is the footing fixed and the instrument audited; does the diagnosis rule out its rivals and reveal something not already known; and does targeting beat untargeted training at matched budget on more than one system.

## Examples
### Accepted lessons
- Auditing which evaluation instruments actually depend on the studied component, before running single-factor comparisons, turns a broad design study into a rigorous one and was rewarded at the top tier despite modest architectural novelty.
- Scaling openly available synthetic data until a capability plateaus reveals precisely where scarce human annotation should be spent, and pairing that annotation with an evaluation of the plateau makes the targeting visible.
- Evaluating each checkpoint with and without an optional aid and partitioning samples by initial correctness decomposes a training gain into named gain and harm terms, and can show the gain is mainly reduced harm rather than new skill.
- Intervening separately on the input-to-intermediate and intermediate-to-output links can expose an intermediate that neither tracks the input nor affects the output, motivating replacement with an explicit, readable form trained on the same source.
- The system's own outcome gap with versus without an auxiliary signal is free supervision for teaching it when to request that signal, avoiding always-on cost.
- Reframing an anti-scaling result as the training objective being satisfiable by a subset of the input, quantified by a usage score, justifies dense per-element supervision that makes the shortcut unprofitable.

### Reject lessons
- A comparative study whose takeaways are not clearly articulated, evaluated on one model family, leaves significance unclear and no reviewer championing it.
- Attributing failure to integration as a residual after component probes is unsafe, because incompletely probed component failures are silently reclassified, and comparative claims without confidence intervals were not accepted.
- Applying existing augmentations or regularizers after a diagnosis, with compute-mismatched baselines and small gains, was judged a marginal contribution whose link between the diagnosed property and the target capability was a logical jump.
- A closed loop in which a strong external generator evolves and filters training problems was read as distillation rather than self-diagnosis, especially with narrow single-domain evaluation.
- A factorial evaluation grid across architectures, tuning scopes and data partitions that offers guidelines but no explanation of why effects occur and no proposed remedy was judged descriptive.
