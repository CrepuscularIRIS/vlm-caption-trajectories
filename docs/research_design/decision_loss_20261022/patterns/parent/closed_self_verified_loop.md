# Close a Self-Generated, Verified Experience Loop
_id: `closed_self_verified_loop`_

**Plain alias**. _Let the system practice, grade itself with a trustworthy checker, and retrain on what it learned_
**Definition**. Have the system propose or explore tasks, label its own successes and failures with an automatic verifier or environment ground truth, and retrain on the harvested experience iteratively without human labels.
**Operational signature**. generate or explore tasks → execute → verify automatically → convert failures/successes into training data → retrain → repeat
**When to apply**. When an environment or verifier can judge outcomes automatically but curated demonstrations are scarce.
**Evidence base**. primary-tagged n=65, oral 0, spotlight 3, poster 42, reject 20; supporting-only n=28 (one of them an oral, ZymCPON45y, not used as primary evidence). The success side is only three spotlights (0bmGL4q7vJ, aA02f0y0LY, iJwDylm93H); posters are used throughout as supporting accepted evidence and are marked as such. The whole corpus has only 13 orals and none is primary here.

## Success conditions (from Oral/Spotlight; Poster as support)
- **The checker draws on objective evidence the learner cannot talk its way past** — executable tool output, live-environment replay, labels that already exist, or ground truth known by construction — rather than the model's own free-text opinion. The tool-grounded verifier of the spotlight aA02f0y0LY is praised specifically for avoiding "evaluation hallucination"; iJwDylm93H verifies recovery by replaying the agent's error prefixes in a live environment; posters make the same move with construction-known edits (s00SNXREV6) and reuse of the dataset's original labels as a containment check (JYV2hrtFSv). [ids: aA02f0y0LY, iJwDylm93H, s00SNXREV6, JYV2hrtFSv]
- **The harvested experience matches the learner's own error and visitation distribution, not a curated or third-party one.** iJwDylm93H (spotlight) branches at the agent's own fragile states so the training data covers the compositional, delayed-detection errors the agent actually makes; poster support anchors rollouts on the base policy's own states before a specialist takes over (eUGoqrZ6Ea), harvests and verifies the agent's own failures online (qup6v4WnYX), and relabels achieved sub-goals of failed runs (9S9V5ntQJM). [ids: iJwDylm93H, eUGoqrZ6Ea, qup6v4WnYX, 9S9V5ntQJM]
- **The loop exploits a real asymmetry inside the system: checking is easier than doing.** aA02f0y0LY reuses the same tools for verifying as for solving; posters use the understanding side to judge the generation side (tVnml9Q4XW), reconstruction from the model's own description as a free verifier (uxea0QCT0e), and the gap between proposing or verifying tasks and executing them (uCkwqAG0uS). The rationale is that a loop only adds information if the grader is reliably better than the actor on the dimension being graded. [ids: aA02f0y0LY, tVnml9Q4XW, uxea0QCT0e, uCkwqAG0uS]
- **The verifier or the measurement is shown to be good in its own right, often becoming an independent deliverable.** aA02f0y0LY's verifier lifts other models as a stand-alone process reward model; 0bmGL4q7vJ shows by ablation that verification matters and adds a human quality study; iJwDylm93H ships a diagnostic benchmark aligned with its synthesized data; posters add verifier–human agreement studies (yKUwkihcsi) and reward-accuracy measurements (xtysskccFc). [ids: aA02f0y0LY, 0bmGL4q7vJ, iJwDylm93H, yKUwkihcsi, xtysskccFc]
- **The benefit of iterating is shown directly, under matched budgets and on more than one backbone.** aA02f0y0LY reports monotonic gains over evolution iterations; iJwDylm93H uses equal-rollout-budget controls; 0bmGL4q7vJ's rebuttal added a second backbone and an open-source generator; posters show iterative over single-stage training (gfX1nqBKtu) and characterize when the loop works (base accuracy above roughly one half, HL1j92hb6z). [ids: aA02f0y0LY, iJwDylm93H, 0bmGL4q7vJ, gfX1nqBKtu, HL1j92hb6z]

## Failure modes (from Reject)
- **The "self" in self-improvement is a stronger external model**, so reviewers read the loop as distillation and the comparison as unfair. A proprietary oracle evolves the problems (JxKkpyRmYU), an external grounding detector supplies the extra supervision (cu8z4V1nJt), a stronger model generates and judges data for a weaker one (IiRlImvLQI), closed models propose and evaluate tasks (v2D1ASk5MT), and a closed rewriter both evolves and eliminates samples (E0dTlxy1T4). [ids: JxKkpyRmYU, cu8z4V1nJt, IiRlImvLQI, v2D1ASk5MT, E0dTlxy1T4]
- **One unvalidated judge does annotation, filtering, reward and evaluation**, so errors are circular and invisible. A single generative reward model drives the whole pipeline (8fswQTV8Dp); model judges supply both reward and evaluation without human checks (i5Y3OD7NQB); metrics depend on an LLM judge with no agreement study (ufi0WPTgWp); the reward depends on an unablated external judge (DdKJL5wHIb). [ids: 8fswQTV8Dp, i5Y3OD7NQB, ufi0WPTgWp, DdKJL5wHIb]
- **A known loop is recombined or grid-searched without a new mechanism**, reading as a tech report. Standard RL techniques transferred with no surprising conclusion (p8UoIVAcU3), a well-known self-training paradigm (5K9XW9gQ5r), a composition of existing generator, models and clustering (oRHmIKfBpq), known attack pieces combined (x1gGnrQEsH), and an extension of known planning schemes (2Qh9YhuElD). [ids: p8UoIVAcU3, 5K9XW9gQ5r, oRHmIKfBpq, x1gGnrQEsH, 2Qh9YhuElD]
- **No matched control isolates the loop from "more data" or a weak baseline.** 447K evolved samples are compared against a 163K seed baseline (E0dTlxy1T4); the competing critic is trained on a tenth of the data and the second flywheel round does not improve steadily (QHDaLyVMCX); the multi-round advantage is unclear, with single-round lowest error in one figure (ufi0WPTgWp); gains may simply be more fine-tuning data (BSBZCa6N3E). [ids: E0dTlxy1T4, QHDaLyVMCX, ufi0WPTgWp, BSBZCa6N3E]
- **Success is shown only on the loop's own proxy or a narrow, aligned setting.** Exploration "success" is only screen change, with no downstream task (IljH2Bt6jO); prompts are aligned with the benchmark (5K9XW9gQ5r); one task and one base model (BSBZCa6N3E); static benchmarks only, beaten by a competitor (kNAQMZf53k); math-only evaluation (JxKkpyRmYU). [ids: IljH2Bt6jO, 5K9XW9gQ5r, BSBZCa6N3E, kNAQMZf53k, JxKkpyRmYU]
- **The learning procedure is mislabelled or under-specified.** The method is called RL but is filtered behaviour cloning with no objective (v2D1ASk5MT); world model, evaluator accuracy and reward settings are unspecified (LrXGarwVO8); policy, "world model" and action space lack formal definitions (2Qh9YhuElD); the self-training framing is judged misleading (cu8z4V1nJt). [ids: v2D1ASk5MT, LrXGarwVO8, 2Qh9YhuElD, cu8z4V1nJt]

## Oral vs Reject gap
Comparing the three spotlights with the 20 rejects, three observable moves separate them. First, the spotlights verify with evidence outside the model's own opinion: executed tool output (aA02f0y0LY), live-environment replay of the agent's own error prefixes (iJwDylm93H), and dedicated query-file and trajectory verifiers whose value is shown by ablation (0bmGL4q7vJ). The rejects most often rest on one unvalidated model judge, or on a stronger external model whose contribution reviewers then classify as distillation. Second, the spotlights measure the thing the loop is supposed to fix and make that measurement a deliverable (a recovery benchmark with controllable error depth; a verifier that works as a stand-alone process reward model), so the loop's gain can be attributed; the rejects report end-benchmark deltas without matched-data or matched-budget controls, or only on the loop's own proxy. Third, the spotlights present one mechanism that explains why the loop adds information (tools verify what text cannot; the agent's own errors define the recovery distribution), while many rejects are recognisable recombinations of STaR- or Evol-style loops. Same-paper pairs in the dossier support this reading: PAE was rejected when the procedure was mislabelled and novelty unclear (v2D1ASk5MT) and later accepted as a poster on its generalization evidence (uCkwqAG0uS); the self-evolve recipe went from reject (p8UoIVAcU3) to poster (X3ikghfWwD) once the exploration-collapse diagnosis carried the paper. With no true orals among the primary papers (n_oral = 0), the oral-only version of this gap cannot be assessed.

## Reviewer expectations
_No primary oral exists for this parent; "oral reviews" below means the success-side (spotlight) reviews, and poster reviews are cited where they repeat the point._
- **Validate the verifier itself: precision against humans, calibration, failure cases. "A model verifies a model" is the first doubt raised.** _(source: both)_ [ids: 0bmGL4q7vJ, aA02f0y0LY, ng4KgSRAW8, 8fswQTV8Dp, i5Y3OD7NQB]
- **Separate genuine self-improvement from distillation of a stronger teacher; claims of "zero external reward" or "self-evolving" are checked against the warm-start and the judge's origin.** _(source: both)_ [ids: aA02f0y0LY, AvBgmOGsdQ, JxKkpyRmYU, cu8z4V1nJt, x7LWdQlwY7]
- **Provide data- and compute-matched controls, including simple baselines such as best-of-n fine-tuning, rejection sampling or uniform sampling.** _(source: both)_ [ids: iJwDylm93H, E0dTlxy1T4, QHDaLyVMCX, GBIUbwW9D8, qup6v4WnYX]
- **Show what happens per round: monotonic gains, plateau or ceiling, error accumulation and failure rates across iterations.** _(source: both)_ [ids: aA02f0y0LY, tVnml9Q4XW, INOi0YqI8p, QHDaLyVMCX, E0dTlxy1T4]
- **Position against the prior loops (STaR, Evol-Instruct, self-rewarding, task proposal) and say what is new beyond composition; even the spotlight iJwDylm93H was asked this.** _(source: both)_ [ids: iJwDylm93H, v2D1ASk5MT, JYV2hrtFSv, nXYedmTf1T, p8UoIVAcU3]
- **Report the loop's cost: rollouts, API calls, wall-clock, and how it scales with depth or number of rounds.** _(source: both)_ [ids: iJwDylm93H, BSBZCa6N3E, Ms9MMN8STj, qE2FyvRvuF, s00SNXREV6]
- **Evaluate outside the loop's own construction: no circular meta-evaluation, no gains measured only on self-built suites, and downstream competence rather than a proxy.** _(source: reject reviews)_ [ids: IljH2Bt6jO, 5K9XW9gQ5r, 31CznLfRIS, qup6v4WnYX]

## Cognitive barriers
- Supervision is assumed to need human labels, ratings or a stronger judge, so a weak model reading its own outcomes (implicit feedback, majority votes, score-conditioned generation, outcome-only judging) looks too noisy to try (BSBZCa6N3E, HL1j92hb6z, 31CznLfRIS, uCkwqAG0uS).
- By default, failed attempts and side branches are discarded. Seeing them as relabellable data, correct prefixes or recovery material reverses the usual filtering convention (Ms9MMN8STj, 2UJrwCrqbw, 9S9V5ntQJM, 8fswQTV8Dp, iJwDylm93H).
- Self-critique inherits the model's own biases. The lever is to anchor the self-signal to evidence the model cannot hallucinate, such as tools, a cross-source similarity term or a reconstruction, which is not visible while the critic is thought of as text-only (aA02f0y0LY, nXYedmTf1T, uxea0QCT0e).
- The natural order is goal first, then data. Exploring first and deriving tasks, queries or labels from what was actually observed (or generating to a target label and keeping matches) inverts it (ng4KgSRAW8, 0bmGL4q7vJ, 31CznLfRIS).
- Loop plateaus are blamed on data or reward quality. Recognising them as collapsing exploration that can be measured and controlled requires treating training dynamics as a signal (X3ikghfWwD, p8UoIVAcU3).
