# Constrain Which Parameters May Change
_id: `constrain_what_changes`_

**Plain alias**. _Constrain which parameters may change_

**Definition**. Prevent forgetting or interference during adaptation by restricting updates to selected parameters or subspaces chosen by importance, orthogonality or separation.

**Operational signature**. estimate importance of existing knowledge per parameter/subspace → mask, orthogonalize or isolate updates → train the new task in the permitted region

**When to apply**. When adapting a capable system to new tasks erodes previously acquired abilities.

**Evidence base**. primary-tagged n=29, oral 0, spotlight 1, poster 17, reject 11; supporting-only n=11. HC not assigned in this run. The success side is a single spotlight (no orals, and only 13 in the whole corpus), so poster papers carry most of the accepted-side evidence below. Supporting-only papers include two spotlights (lFYj0oibGR, CHavqrN1X9), available only in short form.

## Success conditions (from Oral/Spotlight; Poster as support)
confidence: low (top-tier basis is one spotlight; conditions rest mainly on posters)
- **The permitted region follows from a reframing of what kind of change the new task requires, and the paper explains why that region suffices.** — rationale: the spotlight treats adding a new input type as a domain shift, so only the normalization scale-and-shift parameters are tuned, and it backs this with a representation-similarity analysis. Posters that locate the change (an attention-allocation shift around inserted tokens, the cross-modal bridge as the locus of forgetting, the language-backbone component mattering most when merging) are praised for the same diagnosis-first logic. In the supporting-only spotlights, comprehension stays in the frozen pretrained network and only connecting parameters or a removable zero-initialized side module are trained. [ids: YR3ETaElNK, nqWaya7hiX, UfiLIUtbSO, uWJwQ5SZoM; supporting: lFYj0oibGR, CHavqrN1X9]
- **Forgetting is split into separable failures, and each gets its own targeted constraint.** — rationale: accepted papers separate format drift from real knowledge loss, router drift from expert overwriting (shown by freezing experts and retraining only the router), and shallow fact acquisition from unconstrained update directions. Each component then gets a specific constraint (importance-element regularization, subspace-restricted router updates, null-space initialization), and reviewers repeatedly call this decomposition the insight. [ids: teJdFzLnKh, Nvim88fA66, ETBr4m6w4s, zpGK1bOlHt]
- **The constraint adds no parameters or inference cost, or its importance estimate is cheap and needs no original data.** — rationale: the spotlight adds no parameters and cuts memory; posters win with a weight-space interpolation at zero training or inference cost, a mask built from frozen weight magnitude against live gradients with no extra parameters, and a data-free importance probe computed once from self-generated prompts with a stochastic Jacobian estimate. [ids: YR3ETaElNK, uWJwQ5SZoM, FKqmIAnkrb, 20nWnRkLP4, zxg6601zoc]
- **Retention and new-task gain are measured separately, ideally with a balanced metric and a split into in-distribution, out-of-distribution and prior-capability results.** — rationale: reviewers credit a harmonic-mean injection/retention indicator, an evaluation that separates in-distribution, out-of-distribution and generalist performance on real robots, a benchmark variant built to expose essential rather than superficial forgetting, and preserved reasoning alongside new manipulation skill. [ids: ETBr4m6w4s, uWJwQ5SZoM, teJdFzLnKh, tsxwloasw5]
- **The selected region is compared against random or uniform selection and swept over its size.** — rationale: an accepted importance-mask paper is praised for comparing against random masking and sweeping the update ratio. The rejected paper with a similar mask shows a near-flat metric across retention ratios, which reviewers read as evidence that the selection itself may not matter. [ids: 20nWnRkLP4, FKqmIAnkrb, S59bcThbtZ]

## Failure modes (from Reject)
- **The method is a generic continual-learning or parameter-efficient technique applied to a new model class without a design specific to that setting, or it closely matches an existing method.** — rationale: an adaptive moving-average weight that "would apply to text-only models", a null-space constraint resembling prior null-space editing with no direct baseline, activation saliency borrowed from quantization, pruning plus private training combined "in an engineering fashion", and low-rank adapters plus mixture-of-experts already explored in the same setting. [ids: G9qA1JZ0Sy, BDywttV73U, S59bcThbtZ, MKXIYeLMQU, BI0QjdL8PX, 4zfbwpGhd8, KUf2iyin77]
- **The motivating observation is not causally linked to the chosen constraint.** — rationale: higher attention on the underused input stream is not shown to cause better performance (full models do better with lower scores); the causes of the collaboration-underperforms-local phenomenon are never investigated; aligning module convergence is not shown to improve final performance; the claimed task conflict is not justified; and a no-new-method study gives little mechanistic explanation for why forgetting is avoided. [ids: uV9KFBVaFI, BI0QjdL8PX, efLBYwunFF, KUf2iyin77, WLSt5tIOSA]
- **Gains over a budget-matched baseline are marginal or not established.** — rationale: no significant win over a comparable-parameter baseline; marginal gains over the strongest personalized baseline at higher cost; baselines and communication not budget-matched; outdated baselines with marginal gains; results sometimes worse than the vanilla model. [ids: uV9KFBVaFI, BI0QjdL8PX, TNARYxc9YF, 4zfbwpGhd8, KUf2iyin77]
- **Evaluation is too narrow to support a retention claim: one backbone, few tasks, a single step instead of a sequence, or a classification-style setup only.** — rationale: reviewers reject single-backbone, two-task, single-step studies; question-answering-only sequences with omitted benchmark tasks; conclusions resting on one classification-style VQA setup; one main benchmark with the continual benchmark omitted; and scaling laws fitted on two benchmarks. [ids: S59bcThbtZ, G9qA1JZ0Sy, WLSt5tIOSA, BDywttV73U, efLBYwunFF]
- **The constraint rests on a heuristic approximation or an unproven guarantee.** — rationale: a ridge-regression recovery of one low-rank factor without theory; a token-fusion step with only heuristic noise, so the algorithm is not strictly private; norm-based approximations of a second-order weight without empirical support; a lemma that is only a greedy selection argument. [ids: TNARYxc9YF, MKXIYeLMQU, G9qA1JZ0Sy, S59bcThbtZ]

## Oral vs Reject gap
confidence: low (success side is one spotlight plus posters)
The single spotlight differs from the rejects less in the masking machinery than in the reason given for the permitted region. It reframes the new task as a domain shift, restricts training to the parameters a borrowed domain-adaptation finding says suffice (normalization only), and shows the restriction matches or beats full fine-tuning at a fraction of parameters and memory. Accepted posters follow the same route: a diagnostic that splits forgetting into separable parts or locates it in a specific component, then a constraint aimed at that part, evaluated on retention and new-task gain separately. Rejected papers more often import a generic constraint (moving-average merging, activation saliency, null-space initialization, adapter mixtures) without a setting-specific rationale. Their motivating observation is not causally tied to the design, and the gain over a budget-matched or random-selection baseline is marginal or untested. Two near-pairs make the contrast concrete. The rejected and accepted versions of the same knowledge-injection method (BDywttV73U vs ETBr4m6w4s) differ mainly in a balanced retention metric, several backbones and a released dataset. The rejected activation-saliency mask and the accepted importance masks (S59bcThbtZ vs 20nWnRkLP4, FKqmIAnkrb) differ in random-mask controls, ratio sweeps and breadth across models. The oral-only version cannot be assessed: n_oral = 0.

## Cognitive barriers
- Forgetting is treated as a single undifferentiated knowledge loss, so format drift, routing drift and shallow acquisition stay hidden until evaluation or a diagnostic separates them (e.g., teJdFzLnKh, Nvim88fA66, ETBr4m6w4s, WLSt5tIOSA).
- Parameter-efficient tuning is assumed either to need added adapter capacity or to preserve generalization on its own, so the idea that a tiny set of existing parameters suffices, or that most fine-tuned changes are redundant and harmful, goes against expectation (e.g., YR3ETaElNK, rTDyN8yajn, piujJIF3zs).
- Importance-based protection is assumed to need the original training data or expensive second-order information, which hides cheap proxies such as frozen-weight magnitude against live gradients, forward activation statistics, or self-generated probes (e.g., 20nWnRkLP4, FKqmIAnkrb, S59bcThbtZ).
- The granularity of control is taken as fixed (one coefficient per layer, uniform edits across all tokens, a fixed architecture). The lever often sits at a finer or different level: spectral subspaces of the update, one input stream's representations, per-layer sensitivity, or capacity as a convergence-speed control (e.g., F13K6NNeAz, uV9KFBVaFI, zpGK1bOlHt, efLBYwunFF).
- The locus of forgetting is assumed to be the whole backbone. Seeing that it concentrates in the cross-modal bridge, in attention allocation around inserted tokens, or in an action objective overwriting shared weights takes a component-level diagnosis (e.g., UfiLIUtbSO, nqWaya7hiX, tsxwloasw5).
