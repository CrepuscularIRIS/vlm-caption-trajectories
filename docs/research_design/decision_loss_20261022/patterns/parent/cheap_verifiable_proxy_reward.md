# Substitute Cheap Verifiable Signals for Expensive Supervision
_id: `cheap_verifiable_proxy_reward`_

**Plain alias**. _Reward what you can check cheaply_
**Definition**. Replace costly annotated rationales or demonstrations with cheaply checkable proxy, discretized or metric-based rewards, and let reinforcement learning discover the reasoning that satisfies them.
**Operational signature**. identify costly supervision → find a cheap checkable proxy (label, choice, match, metric) → reformulate task to be verifiable → optimize with reward instead of imitation
**When to apply**. When the target capability is hard to annotate step by step but its outcomes, or a proxy of them, are cheap to verify.
**Evidence base**. primary-tagged n=89, oral 1, spotlight 4, poster 55, reject 29; supporting-only n=74

_Note on the evidence: the success side (oral ∪ spotlight) is only 5 papers, and there is a single oral (the whole corpus has 13). Poster papers are used below as supporting accepted evidence wherever the 5 success papers alone are too thin, and this is marked in each bullet. HC labels were not assigned in this run._

## Success conditions (from Oral/Spotlight; Poster as support)
- **The reward is shaped to the structure of the target judgment — relative, graded, partial-credit or set-valued — rather than a binary exact match** — accepted papers that reward ordering through a probabilistic comparison model, kernelized closeness for continuous values, or order-invariant matching for multi-item outputs get credit for a "novel formulation" even when the optimizer is stock; the reward itself carries the insight about what a near-miss is. Posters add overlap-based interval rewards, joint absolute-plus-rank rewards and bipartite-matching rewards with the same praise. [ids: uL7lCOHtiZ, AYy0lOycql, Bds54EfR9x; poster support: or3ZukbrKw, gJ05Gm5VxQ, BeXcXrXetA, asec7Zx8C6]
- **The checkable signal is moved into a symbolic or rule-parseable space, so verification is exact and cheap** — the oral parses generated states into discrete actions with a rule-based interpreter and scores progress with a search-based estimator; a spotlight turns abstract safety requirements into symbolic predicates that yield per-step costs. Posters make a verifier's rationale a bounding box scored by overlap, or decompose free text into extracted fact units. Moving the check into a machine-readable form is what lets a capability with no natural ground truth become verifiable. [ids: wsnse46kRO, dt940loCBT; poster support: e1bpZMpANm, 6YVSHa3g4b, OAXECnLxuk]
- **The proxy unlocks a capability or paradigm that imitation could not reach, and the paper frames it that way** — success-side papers present the verifiable signal as enabling something new (planning with no language at all, safety as a constrained objective, reasoning that emerges from score-only labels), not as "the same recipe on new data". Posters that manufacture verifiability where none existed (corrupt-and-localize, blind-consumer question answering, ordering pretexts) receive the same framing praise. [ids: wsnse46kRO, dt940loCBT, Bds54EfR9x; poster support: j42rziWq1n, JLelnhqXaC, tBf2SUzfZw]
- **Label-only or proxy-only reward produces measured out-of-distribution or cross-dataset gains, not just in-distribution scores** — the spotlights in quality assessment and emotion assessment are praised for strong out-of-distribution and cross-domain generalization and for pooling datasets with different scales; posters show transfer from a proxy task (games, cross-view matching, self-supervised pretexts) to unrelated benchmarks. Generalization is the evidence that the cheap signal taught the capability rather than the proxy. [ids: Bds54EfR9x, uL7lCOHtiZ, AYy0lOycql; poster support: u1tsgXPh2o, MJBAmHLr8E, vWNZT9waPN]
- **The contribution of the reward stage is isolated from warm-start data, extra data and base-model strength** — accepted papers that establish a saturated supervised baseline, show gains without trajectory supervision, or ablate reasoning-RL apart from tool retraining turn reviewer skepticism into acceptance; on the success side the same attribution question is raised and answered in rebuttal. This rests mostly on poster evidence. [ids: Bds54EfR9x, dt940loCBT; poster support: 14qYxv2jki, 3kouij8BWi, H7gtryDnVK]
- **The proxy's validity is checked by something other than the proxy itself (human study, external metric, alternative judge)** — posters that added a human study, human rationale evaluation or expert ranking after a judge-based reward moved to acceptance; spotlight reviewers still flag the absence of such checks as the main residual weakness. Mostly poster evidence. [ids: AYy0lOycql, Bds54EfR9x; poster support: JLelnhqXaC, gXjOsBcXIR, 6YVSHa3g4b]

## Failure modes (from Reject)
- **Applying a standard warm-start-then-policy-gradient recipe with coarse format-plus-accuracy rewards to a new domain or dataset** — the most frequent rejection reason: reviewers read the work as "direct application of an existing RL algorithm" with no reward or algorithmic insight, regardless of strong numbers. [ids: kHitmBK3M3, nJam0e7wvJ, 5WJzbZZeh1, dFmG4cOC22, ExSJgAanCO, sj9jmrBjMf, uO2WfDYfeK, Wf3EbtqGdw]
- **The same metric, encoder or judge is used both as the reward and as the evaluation** — circularity makes gains look like metric alignment rather than capability; reviewers ask for an external grounding metric or human assessment and reject when none is given. [ids: 32QQlzm9ft, pK2636Prbq, ExSJgAanCO, XFY7kvIFSw, EPva1hwEz9]
- **The proxy can be satisfied by a degenerate change in output length or style, and this is not ruled out** — fewer hallucinated objects from shorter captions, higher judge scores from longer reports, or overlong intent-ignoring answers signal reward exploitation; experts rating the top-scoring method lowest is decisive. [ids: bO31lfEdos, pK2636Prbq, B6t5wy6g5a]
- **Gains cannot be attributed because of confounds in data, teachers, test-time compute or comparison settings** — distillation from stronger teachers, training on the evaluation pool, entangled test-time search, or differently sourced baseline numbers leave the central claim unisolated. [ids: 7HT5i6w6qU, Hghm8tVvbs, P6SlbFL9IF, YqejpUv7IK, LzycEbgLoi]
- **A central behavioral or causal claim is asserted but never formalized or tested against alternatives** — "emergent reflection" measured only by keyword counts, or "missing alignment causes the deficit" without ruling out pre-training or instruction-tuning explanations, is read as unsupported. [ids: CaIoemPKp0, SrkDVzygXx, YqejpUv7IK, QpYBbBZHoF]
- **Narrow empirical scope: one small backbone, one task, synthetic or bespoke benchmark** — reviewers doubt the proxy generalizes when only one setting is tested, and added rebuttal experiments rarely suffice. [ids: bO31lfEdos, kHitmBK3M3, 3fuPS85ekI, wYgtymmOFC, CaIoemPKp0, nJam0e7wvJ]
- **Missing comparison with the closest verifier-, reward- or trigger-based prior work** — when a close body of work exists, an unpositioned method is rejected even with gains. [ids: 2aCaXcalRZ, Rr8gAjB1dX, OoChIYXsfA, sj9jmrBjMf]

## Oral vs Reject gap
On the success side (1 oral, 4 spotlights) the cheap signal is itself the idea: the reward encodes how the target should be judged (relative ranking under a comparison model, kernelized partial credit, symbolic safety predicates, rule-parsed state transitions), and the paper frames the result as a new formulation or paradigm, backed by out-of-distribution or cross-dataset evidence. On the reject side the cheap signal is an off-the-shelf format-plus-accuracy or metric-overlap reward bolted onto a standard warm-start-then-RL pipeline for a new domain, often with the reward metric reused for evaluation, an unchecked length/style shortcut, or confounded attribution. Notably, success-side reviews still call the optimizer incremental (spotlight concerns include "natural combination of established" methods and "common" SFT plus RL), so what separates the sides is the reward formulation and the framing, not algorithmic novelty. With only one true oral (n_oral = 1), the oral-only version of this gap cannot be assessed.

## Reviewer expectations
_"oral reviews" here means success-side (oral ∪ spotlight) reviews._
- **Show that the reward formulation, not just the optimizer, is the contribution; a reward that captures the structure of the judgment is praised as a novel formulation, while plain policy-gradient on a new dataset is called limited novelty** _(source: both)_ [ids: uL7lCOHtiZ, wsnse46kRO, Bds54EfR9x, kHitmBK3M3, nJam0e7wvJ, 5WJzbZZeh1]
- **Validate the proxy independently of itself — human study, inter-rater agreement, external metric or alternative judge — especially when a judge model supplies the reward** _(source: both)_ [ids: AYy0lOycql, Bds54EfR9x, 32QQlzm9ft, pK2636Prbq, XFY7kvIFSw, EPva1hwEz9]
- **Isolate where gains come from: reward stage versus warm-start data, extra joint-training data, teacher distillation, base model or test-time compute** _(source: both)_ [ids: Bds54EfR9x, 7HT5i6w6qU, P6SlbFL9IF, YqejpUv7IK, ExSJgAanCO]
- **Ablate reward components and report sensitivity to hand-set weights, thresholds and kernels** _(source: both)_ [ids: AYy0lOycql, dt940loCBT, XFY7kvIFSw, 5qfX9zJDi2, wYgtymmOFC]
- **Test beyond one backbone, one task and simulation; demonstrate the proxy carries over to harder or real settings** _(source: both)_ [ids: wsnse46kRO, dt940loCBT, bO31lfEdos, kHitmBK3M3, CaIoemPKp0]
- **Report the compute and latency cost of multi-sample rewards, rendering or multi-pass inference** _(source: both)_ [ids: uL7lCOHtiZ, 5qfX9zJDi2, G0ettIR8yv, P6SlbFL9IF]
- **Rule out reward exploitation through output length or style before claiming the target behavior improved** _(source: reject reviews)_ [ids: bO31lfEdos, pK2636Prbq, B6t5wy6g5a]
- **Data efficiency and out-of-distribution generalization from label-only reward are what reviewers reward most explicitly** _(source: oral reviews)_ [ids: Bds54EfR9x, uL7lCOHtiZ, AYy0lOycql]

## Cognitive barriers
- Verifiable-reward training is assumed to need tasks with naturally checkable answers such as math or code; perception, description and continuous-control targets look either too ambiguous or too easy to score, so nobody thinks to manufacture verifiability by corruption, discretization, a blind consumer or a decode-and-score pipeline. [ids: j42rziWq1n, JLelnhqXaC, N2bLuwofZ0, t6h8LhOupF]
- The default supervision target (a reference text, an exact timestamp, a single regression value, a token-level loss) looks natural, which hides that it penalizes near-misses as harshly as errors or ignores the relative, one-to-many or order-invariant structure of the target. [ids: gJ05Gm5VxQ, uL7lCOHtiZ, ea1U1MgbdT, Wf3EbtqGdw]
- Existing artifacts are seen in their usual role — self-supervised pretexts as pre-training tools, frozen encoders and segmenters as feature extractors or inference tools, layer contrasts as decoding tricks, metadata as irrelevant — rather than as reward sources. [ids: vWNZT9waPN, tBf2SUzfZw, cJQ1K2fjpD, Smm2KRLqx5, dHOSTp8MBl]
- That training on an unrelated-looking proxy (games, cross-view matching, perception pretexts) transfers to the target capability is counterintuitive, because reasoning post-training is assumed to need domain-matched data. [ids: u1tsgXPh2o, MJBAmHLr8E, lbRi2aAWL7, nJam0e7wvJ]
- The verify-versus-produce asymmetry is easy to miss: checking a reconstruction, a round-trip through an inverse model, or the likelihood of observed behavior is far cheaper than producing a faithful annotation, yet the field reaches for paired annotations first. [ids: 5qfX9zJDi2, 2Twzlf6qFv, FcGQVshxMP, efbZ2Qe2D8]
